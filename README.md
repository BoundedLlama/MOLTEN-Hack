StageReady VR by Rishi, Yuhaen, and Karl

StageReady VR is a VR rehearsal room where you upload your slides, face a simulated audience, answer live spoken questions from an AI audience member, and get coaching afterward based on where your head was pointing. It solves the problem that most people only practice presentations alone in front of a screen. Built with React, Three.js, WebXR and PDF.js on a Meta Quest 3S, with ElevenLabs for the voice Q&A and AssemblyAI for the coaching; live reminders, hand grabbing and speech scoring aren’t built yet.
